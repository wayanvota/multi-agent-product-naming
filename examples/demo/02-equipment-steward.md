# Equipment Steward

Fictional primary persona. This person maintains shared tools and makes sure session organizers know which equipment is available. They care about realistic preparation times and clear ownership of follow-up tasks.

They want fewer last-minute promises that depend on equipment being ready. They worry that a cheerful booking interface could conceal unresolved maintenance or missing training. The scheduling tool must leave qualification and safety decisions to responsible people.

They prefer names suggesting competence, practical organization, and honest limits. A playful name can work, but a name implying effortless manufacture or certified safety would reduce trust. Their adoption test is whether an organizer can understand a constraint and find a workable alternative.
