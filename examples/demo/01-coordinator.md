# Workshop Coordinator

Fictional primary persona. This person organizes several recurring sessions and finds changes difficult to communicate consistently. They want a single view of rooms, resources, and named responsibilities.

Their motivation is a calmer start to each session. Their anxiety is discovering that an instructor and a group have arrived for a room already in use. They want software that helps resolve a conflict without creating a second administrative job.

They respond to names suggesting clear coordination and dependable preparation. They distrust grand automation promises. They would recommend a tool when its name is easy to pronounce and explain to a volunteer in a busy room. Technical sophistication is less persuasive than a visible reduction in scheduling confusion.
