# Volunteer Host

Fictional primary persona. This person hosts a session occasionally and does not live in the scheduling system. They want to know when to arrive, what needs preparation, and whom to contact when plans change.

Their motivation is a welcoming session with enough time to help participants. Their anxiety is being assigned responsibility they did not understand or agree to. They prefer clear invitations and explicit acceptance of duties.

They like names that sound approachable and are easy to recognize in a message. An industrial or managerial name may feel intended for paid administrators. A name that promises a social network could create the wrong expectation. They would engage when the tool makes the next task obvious without demanding extensive setup.
